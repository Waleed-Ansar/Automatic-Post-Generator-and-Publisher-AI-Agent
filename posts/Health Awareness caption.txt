Hypertension 2025: Updated Guidelines

Know Your Numbers

• Normal: <120/80 mmHg
• Elevated: 120-129/<80
• Stage 1 HTN: 130-139/80-89
• Stage 2 HTN: ≥140/≥90
• Crisis: >180/>120
• Treatment target: <130/80 for ALL adults
• ESC 2024: New Elevated BP category (120-139/70-89)
• ~47% of US adults affected (~120 million)
• Early detection saves lives

Check your BP today. Know your numbers. ❤️
