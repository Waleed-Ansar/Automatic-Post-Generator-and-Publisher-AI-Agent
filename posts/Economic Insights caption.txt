📈 Inflation Surges to 3.8%

The Highest Level Since May 2023

• US inflation jumped from 2.4% to 3.8% in 2026
• Iran war disrupted oil trade via Strait of Hormuz
• Gasoline prices surged ~28% year-over-year
• Energy costs drove nearly half of April CPI
• Housing, food, and money supply also contributed
• Fed now faces tough battle above 2% target
